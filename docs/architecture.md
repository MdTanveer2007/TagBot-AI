# TagBot AI Architecture

## 1. Vision

TagBot AI is a long-term personal AI companion for Tanveer.

It should act as:

- A friendly study partner
- A technical co-founder
- An AI engineering mentor
- A personal second brain
- A productivity and planning assistant

The goal is to gradually build TagBot AI from a simple assistant into a capable personal AI system with memory, tools, knowledge retrieval, automation, and a custom interface.
